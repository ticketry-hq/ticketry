# Official guidance for a Show HN post

Research snapshot: 2026-08-21. Sources are limited to first-party Hacker News
and Y Combinator pages. This note covers ordinary Show HN submissions, not the
curated Launch HN program for YC startups.

## The short version

A Show HN is for a non-trivial thing you personally made and can let readers
try. It may be early and visually rough, but it must exist, work well enough to
explore, and have a person behind it who will join the discussion. A landing
page, waitlist, fundraiser, article, newsletter, list, or quick generated
one-off does not qualify. Make access easy, ideally without signup or email
verification. ([Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html))

The current HN guidelines prohibit generated or AI-edited comments. The
moderator's Show HN tips go further: write the submission text by hand and do
not use an LLM to generate, edit, or polish any part of it. This is the first
constraint to put into the launch process. Research and factual checks can
support the author, but the words posted to HN must be the author's own.
Treat the title, submission text, and founder comments as covered by that
boundary.
([HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html), [Show HN
presentation tips](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22336638))

## Who and what qualifies

- The submitter must have worked on the project personally and be available to
  discuss it. The project should be substantial enough to reflect real work,
  though HN explicitly accepts early-stage projects that are not polished.
  ([Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html))
- Readers need a way to use or inspect the actual work. Software should be
  runnable or otherwise interactive. Hardware may use a video or detailed
  article, and a book may use a sample chapter. ([Show HN
  rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html))
- Remove trial barriers when possible. A signup page by itself is not a Show
  HN, and a usable product hidden behind unnecessary signup or email steps will
  receive less feedback. ([Show HN
  rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html), [Show HN presentation
  tips](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22336638))
- Blog posts, newsletters, curated lists, other reading-only material,
  fundraisers, market tests, and landing pages belong in a regular submission
  or are not ready to post. ([Show HN
  rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html), [Show HN presentation
  tips](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22336638))
- A routine feature release or incremental version is not enough. A major
  overhaul or a release with significant new information may qualify.
  ([Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html), [Show HN
  presentation tips](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22336638))

HN currently has a temporary restriction notice for would-be posters who are
not yet familiar with the community. It asks them to spend time on the site,
become good contributors, and then post Show HNs occasionally. The notice does
not give an account-age, karma, or activity threshold, so there is no published
number to target. ([current Show HN restriction
notice](https://news.ycombinator.com/showlim), [HN welcome
page](https://news.ycombinator.com/newswelcome.html))

This fits the broader rule that a person may submit their own work sometimes,
but should not use HN mainly for promotion. The moderator tips also recommend a
personal username rather than a company or product name. ([HN
guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html), [Show HN
presentation tips](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22336638))

## Title and destination

The title must begin with `Show HN`. The established form is `Show HN: ...`.
Keep the rest concrete and descriptive. HN's general title rules reject
attention tricks such as all caps, exclamation points, praise, gratuitous
numbers, linkbait, and editorializing. They also say to omit a site name when
HN will already display it after the link. ([Show HN
rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html), [HN
guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html), [current Show HN
listing](https://news.ycombinator.com/show))

The Show HN rules do not publish a separate character limit. The 80-character
title rule on the Launch HN instruction page belongs to the curated YC Launch
HN format, so it should not be presented as a documented Show HN requirement.
Its underlying advice still helps: a short, factual description is a better fit
than a sales tagline. ([Launch HN
instructions](https://news.ycombinator.com/yli.html), [Show HN
rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html))

Point the submission at the primary place where readers can try or inspect the
project, not a press article or promotional intermediary. HN asks submitters to
use the original source, and Show HN specifically rejects a mere landing page.
If the usable demonstration is a video or PDF, append `[video]` or `[pdf]` to
the title as the general guidelines require. ([HN
guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html), [Show HN
rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html))

Add a handwritten explanation with the submission. The moderator tips say it
should appear above the discussion for a Show HN; if it does not, add the same
context as the first comment. HN's FAQ likewise treats a regular comment as the
way to accompany a link submission with commentary. Verify the live thread
after submitting instead of assuming the text rendered. ([Show HN presentation
tips](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22336638), [HN
FAQ](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsfaq.html))

## What the explanation should cover

HN's own guidance consistently asks for factual, personal, technical writing.
The explanation should cover:

- a clear statement of what the project does, phrased differently from the
  title;
- the problem, why it mattered to the author, and the backstory that led to the
  work;
- how the solution works, with enough technical detail for builders to examine
  it;
- what differs from existing approaches;
- relevant earlier HN discussions;
- how the project makes money or might make money, plus transparent pricing
  where applicable;
- an open invitation for experiences, ideas, and criticism.

([Show HN presentation tips](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22336638),
[Launch HN instructions](https://news.ycombinator.com/yli.html))

The Launch HN writing guide is formally for YC launches, but it explicitly says
non-YC startups should follow its writing advice. It recommends the founder's
own voice, plain facts, modest language, and technical depth. It warns against
corporate copy, superlatives, unexplained jargon, feature inventories,
single-sentence sales paragraphs, and pitch-deck language. ([Launch HN
instructions](https://news.ycombinator.com/yli.html))

## Posting day and discussion

Be free to participate when the post goes live. Reply as comments arrive and do
not disappear for long stretches. Give generous detail and answer directly.
Treat criticism as useful input, stay cheerful and non-defensive, and avoid
side conversations with friends that make other readers feel excluded.
([Launch HN instructions](https://news.ycombinator.com/yli.html), [HN
guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html))

Do not ask friends, users, coworkers, or any audience to upvote, comment, or
submit the project. Do not arrange booster comments. HN says it may penalize or
ban submissions, accounts, and sites for vote solicitation, and readers often
flag coordinated comments as spam. A friend or fan may comment if they have
something substantive and non-promotional to add, but not as launch support.
([Show HN
rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html), [HN
FAQ](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsfaq.html), [Show HN presentation
tips](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22336638))

HN does not promise placement. Every Show HN appears on `newest` and `shownew`.
It reaches the `show` page only after a small points threshold. Story rank also
depends on age, flags, anti-abuse systems, discussion downweighting, account or
site weighting, and moderator action. Higher account karma does not by itself
raise a submission's rank. ([Show HN
rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html), [HN
FAQ](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsfaq.html))

The `show` page is not the HN front page. Ordinary Show HNs compete for front
page rank like other submissions. Automatic front page placement is a separate
benefit for curated YC Launch HN posts. ([HN
FAQ](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsfaq.html), [Launch HN
instructions](https://news.ycombinator.com/yli.html))

The official Show HN pages do not recommend a weekday or hour. The useful
scheduling rule is therefore personal availability, not an invented timing
formula. The Launch HN page's advice about avoiding coordination with press is
inside its YC-only logistics section, so it should not be treated as a binding
Show HN rule. ([Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html),
[Launch HN instructions](https://news.ycombinator.com/yli.html))

## Reposts and failed attempts

Do not delete and repost the same submission. For ordinary stories, the FAQ
allows a small number of reposts when the story has not received significant
attention in roughly the past year. The more specific Show HN advice says a new
release should be significantly different, should link the earlier Show HN and
explain the change, and should normally recur no more than once or twice a
year. ([HN FAQ](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsfaq.html), [HN
guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html), [Show HN
presentation tips](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=22336638))

A post missing from `show` has not necessarily been moderated. It may simply
not have crossed the points threshold. A `[flagged]` label means users or a
moderator judged that it broke the guidelines or did not belong; `[dead]` means
software, flags, or moderators killed it. The FAQ asks users who believe a dead
post is valid to use the site's vouch mechanism rather than delete and resubmit.
([HN FAQ](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsfaq.html))

## Preflight checklist

- The project is live and a stranger can reach the useful part.
- The submitter personally worked on it and can answer detailed questions.
- The HN account belongs to a participating person, not a product identity.
- The author has handwritten a title that starts with `Show HN:` and states
  what the project is without hype.
- The URL goes to the runnable project, source repository, or honest demo.
- Signup, confirmation, and onboarding barriers are removed where possible,
  and unavoidable barriers are disclosed.
- Pricing and the business model are visible or explained where relevant.
- The author has handwritten the explanation and checked it for clear product,
  problem, backstory, technical, differentiation, and feedback sections.
- Earlier HN threads are linked if this is a substantial new version.
- Nobody has been asked to vote or add a launch-day comment.
- The author has reserved time to stay with the thread and answer criticism.
