PILLAR 1 · STORE DECOUPLING (R2)

Pure Selectors & Facades

Clean up `backlogStore.ts` (42KB) by isolating selectors and extracting heavy mutation action logic.

  • Extract pure selectors (`groupBacklog`, `boardColumns`, `boardSwimlanes`, etc.) to `stores/selectors/`
  • Move complex mutation helper actions to external helper files
  • Keep `useBacklogStore` Zustand facade API fully backward compatible
PILLAR 2 · COMPONENT DECOMPOSITION (R3 & R5)

Backlog & Board Splits

Break down monolith components like `BacklogList.tsx` and normalize swimlane rendering boundaries.

  • Extract `Row` and `InlineCreateRow` out of `BacklogList.tsx` to `views/backlog/`
  • Decompose collapsible state localstorage utility methods
  • Normalize Kanban board card, column, and swimlane prop interfaces
PILLAR 3 · STRUCTURE & FIELDS (R4 & R6 & R7)

Settings & Shared Pickers

Organize settings panels, normalize pickers/fields and standardise overlay stacking order.

  • Extract issue type and state panels from `SettingsView.tsx` into `shell/settings/`
  • Normalize Radix Popover and custom picker components under `fields/`
  • Verify NavDrawer, Modal, and Toast stack layers in `shell/`
